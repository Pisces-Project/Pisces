*************************************
Astrophysical Models
*************************************

This section contains examples showcasing the construction and analysis of complete astrophysical models.
These are end-to-end workflows that typically involve density profiles, temperature or pressure modeling,
and often magnetic fields or particle distributions.

Each example is intended to demonstrate a realistic scientific use case where `Pisces` can be applied.

Examples are sorted by their model type.
